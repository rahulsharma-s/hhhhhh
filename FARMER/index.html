THE CHALLENGE

Every step in the field
should feel safer.

Traditional navigation tools may not provide enough protection for visually impaired farmers working in challenging agricultural environments.

🌧️

Muddy Terrain

Soft soil, slippery paths and uneven ground can make movement difficult and increase the risk of falling.

⚠️

Hidden Obstacles

Stones, branches, tools and other objects can create hazards while moving through a farm.

📶

Poor Connectivity

Internet-based assistance may not be practical in remote locations with unreliable or nonexistent internet access.

OUR CONCEPT

Assistance that works
closer to the farmer.

DrishtiFarm proposes an assistive navigation device that processes sensor information locally and provides simple alerts to help users identify potential hazards.

  • ✓ Designed with offline operation in mind
  • ✓ Potential obstacle detection using local sensors
  • ✓ Audio or vibration alert concept
  • ✓ Intended for rural agricultural environments
